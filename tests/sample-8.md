# Q3 2024 Product Performance Report

Prepared by the Product Analytics team — October 2024

## Executive Summary

Q3 2024 saw a **23% increase** in monthly active users, driven primarily by the launch
of the mobile app redesign in July. Revenue grew by 18% quarter-over-quarter, reaching €2.4M.
Customer churn improved from 4.2% to 3.1%.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Q2 2024 | Q3 2024 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly Active Users | 84,200 | 103,600 | +23% |
| Revenue (€) | 2,034,000 | 2,400,000 | +18% |
| Churn Rate | 4.2% | 3.1% | -1.1pp |
| NPS Score | 42 | 51 | +9 |
| Avg. Session Duration | 4m 12s | 5m 38s | +34% |
| Support Tickets | 1,840 | 1,210 | -34% |

## Top Features by Usage

1. Dashboard (viewed by 91% of users daily)
2. Export to CSV (new in Q3 — adopted by 67% of paid accounts)
3. Collaboration comments (48% of teams)
4. API integration (38% of enterprise accounts)

## Incidents

A **6-hour outage** occurred on 14 September due to a database failover issue.
Affected approximately 12,000 users. Post-mortem published internally on 18 September.

## Q4 Priorities

* Launch AI-powered report summaries
* Complete SOC 2 Type II audit
* Expand to 3 new EU markets
* Reduce p95 API latency from 420ms to under 200ms
